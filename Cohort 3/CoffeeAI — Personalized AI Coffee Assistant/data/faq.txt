# CoffeeAI Knowledge Base & FAQ Grounding Document

## 1. Coffee Sourcing & Roasting Philosophy
- Bean Origins: We directly source from ethical, specialty-grade co-ops in Ethiopia (Yirgacheffe & Guji), Colombia (Huila Gesha), Guatemala (Antigua), and Panama (Boquete).
- Roasting Schedule: All beans are small-batch roasted on our Loring S35 Kestrel convection roaster at our Downtown Roastery twice weekly. Coffee is served between 4 to 14 days post-roast for peak flavor development.
- Organic & Fair Trade: 100% of our coffee beans are certified organic, shade-grown, and bought at minimum 40% above fair trade minimum prices.

## 2. Cold Brew & Nitro Methods
- Signature Cold Brew Extraction: 20-hour cold water immersion at 38°F (3.3°C) using coarse-ground Ethiopian Yirgacheffe. This yields exceptionally low acidity (67% less acid than hot brew) and natural dark cocoa notes without bitterness.
- Nitro Velvet Draft: Nitrogen gas is infused under high pressure into chilled cold brew kegs, dispensed through a stout restrictor faucet. It produces a velvety stout-like foam head with zero added dairy or sugar.
- Cold Brew Shelf Life: Brewed fresh daily; maximum hold time is 48 hours in refrigerated kegs.

## 3. Milk & Plant-Based Alternatives
- Oat Milk: We exclusively serve Oatly Barista Edition for our oat milk drinks. It foams microfoam identically to dairy milk with no added seed oils or gums.
- Almond Milk: Califia Farms Barista Blend (unsweetened, gluten-free, soy-free).
- Dairy Milks: Organic Clover Sonoma Whole Milk and Organic Skim Milk from pasture-raised local California dairy farms.
- Coconut & Soy Milk: Organic Pacific Foods Barista series.
- Cross-Contamination: We use separate steam wands and color-coded frothing pitchers for dairy and non-dairy plant milks.

## 4. Sweetness & Customizations
- House Syrups: All syrups (Madagascar Bourbon Vanilla, Lavender Honey, Salted Butterscotch Caramel, Spiced Cardamom) are made in-house daily from real cane sugar and natural botanicals. We never use high-fructose corn syrup or artificial preservatives.
- Sweetness Adjustments: Customers can request 0% (Unsweetened), 25% (Quarter sweet), 50% (Half sweet), 75% (Three-quarter sweet), or 100% (Standard).
- Natural Sweeteners: Raw Wildflower Honey, Blue Agave Nectar, and Monk Fruit Organic Drops (0 calories, keto-friendly) are available upon request at no charge.

## 5. Caffeine & Decaffeination Process
- Swiss Water Decaf Process: Our decaffeinated espresso and filter coffees use the 100% chemical-free Swiss Water process. It removes 99.9% of caffeine using pure British Columbia water and green coffee extract, preserving full origin flavors and leaving only 2–5mg caffeine per serving.
- High Caffeine Options: Signature Cold Brew (210mg/16oz), Nitro Draft (240mg/16oz).
- Low Caffeine Options: Kyoto Ceremonial Matcha (80mg/16oz), Citrus Espresso Tonic (150mg/14oz).

## 6. Dietary & Allergen Guide
- Gluten-Free: All black coffees, cold brews, and teas are naturally 100% gluten-free. Oat milk used is certified certified gluten-free.
- Vegan Drinks: Signature Cold Brew, Nitro Cold Brew, Panama Gesha Pour-Over, Citrus Espresso Tonic, Oat Vanilla Latte (with oat milk), Matcha Latte (with plant milk).
- Keto / Low Carb: Cold Brew, Nitro Draft, Americano, Cortado with Almond milk, or any drink sweetened with Monk Fruit drops.
- Nut Allergies: Almond Croissant contains tree nuts (almonds). We prepare it in a separate bakery case to avoid contact with drinks.
